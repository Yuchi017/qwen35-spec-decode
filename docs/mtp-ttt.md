# Aligning MTP finetuning with vLLM's MTP proposer

speculators finetunes a model's native MTP head following FastMTP:

- **Step 0:** the MTP layer runs over the whole sequence on `[verifier hidden_t ; embed(token_{t+1})]`.
- **Step k:** it runs over the whole sequence again, on the previous step's outputs and the ground-truth embeddings shifted by k.
- **Loss:** exponentially decayed per-step weights.

vLLM's MTP proposer, which actually serves the head, differs in two ways.

## 1. Attention pattern (TTT)

In vLLM, draft step k for an anchor t:
- sits at position t + k;
- attends to the **step-0 KV** of positions ≤ t, which was computed from verifier hidden states when the context was processed;
- attends to the KV of its **own** earlier draft steps for that anchor.

It never sees other anchors' draft chains. The FastMTP-style recursion instead lets step k attend to step-(k−1) outputs at every position: a sequence-wide recursion that does not exist at inference.

The patch adds `_forward_ttt`, which reproduces vLLM's pattern with a flex-attention block mask:
- the KV is `[step-0 KV | step-1 KV | … ]`;
- the query for anchor t may see step-0 keys at positions ≤ t in the same document, plus its own chain's keys;
- each step uses its own position offset.

## 2. Pre- vs. post-final-norm hidden states (POSTNORM)

speculators extracts the verifier's last-layer hidden states via vLLM's aux-hidden-state capture, which gives `hidden + residual` before the final norm. vLLM's MTP proposer instead feeds the head the verifier's output hidden states, after the final RMSNorm. The patch applies the verifier's final norm before the MTP layer. The norm weight is read from the verifier checkpoint, so it is neither trained nor saved, and Qwen3.5's zero-centred norm `(1 + w)` is handled.

## Enable

```bash
SPECULATORS_MTP_TTT=1 SPECULATORS_MTP_POSTNORM=1 \
torchrun --standalone --nproc_per_node 1 -m speculators.train --speculator-type mtp \
  --verifier-name-or-path Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B --target-layer-ids 32 \
  --num-speculative-steps 8 --step-weight-beta 0.8 --total-seq-len 12288 \
  --lr 1e-5 --gradient-checkpointing ...
```

The patch also includes:
- **Memory:** `lm_head` + cross-entropy are evaluated only at supervised positions and recomputed in backward. Full logits for 8 steps × 12k tokens × 248k vocabulary would need tens of GB. With `supports_gradient_checkpointing` on the MTP model, 8 steps at 12,288 tokens fit on one 48 GB L40S, at ~2.7 s per step.
- **Metric:** `accept_pos_k`, the share of anchors whose steps 1..k are all correct under teacher forcing. Teacher-forced inputs equal the drafted tokens whenever all earlier steps are correct, so this equals vLLM's `spec_decode_num_accepted_tokens_per_pos / num_drafts`.

## Results (internal model)

Starting from the native head (online accept length 4.37), with 8 draft steps, β=0.8 and lr 1e-5:

| Training forward | Online accept length (K=5) |
|---|---|
| Native head (no finetune) | 4.37 |
| FastMTP-style (upstream) | **3.92** (worse than not training) |
| TTT + POSTNORM | **~4.6** |

With TTT + POSTNORM, offline `accept_pos_k` on the validation split tracks online vLLM per-position accept within about 1 percentage point. The upstream forward has no such correspondence.
